# Planets and moons

- `empyrion_system_info` lists a known system's bodies with type and difficulty. With a planet or moon name it gives
  that body's details: the game's description, special effects, gravity, atmosphere, oxygen, breathable or not,
  temperatures, radiation,
  resources. Only systems the player or their faction discovered; others show star class and territory only.
- Retell the description in your own words and pick what matters for the player's plan.

## What the values mean

- **Difficulty** (Easy, Medium, Hard, …) is the game's own rating of the planet's dangers.
- **Special effects** (e.g. "Sensor Interference: POI detection range reduced") change how the planet plays; mention
  them when they matter.
- **Gravity**: above about 1.2 g heavy ships need a lot of lift thrust to take off again; warn before landing a big ship.
  Below 0.5 g jumps and landings are floaty.
- **Breathable** is the game's own yes/no: on a breathable planet the suit uses no oxygen; on all others it does.
- **Temperature and radiation**: extremes need armor boosts or a quick visit.
- **Resources**: what the planet can have; where exactly needs scanning in the game.
